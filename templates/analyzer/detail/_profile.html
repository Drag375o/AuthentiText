How to read this

Each score rescales one measured feature between two fixed anchors, listed in its explanation. A high score means high within that range, not higher than other people.

There is no “good” profile. Formal writing scores high on formality; a diary scores high on personal voice. Neither is better.

Feature scores, not personality judgments.
